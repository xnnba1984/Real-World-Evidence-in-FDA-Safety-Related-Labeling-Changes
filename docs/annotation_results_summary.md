## Interpretation

The main pattern is not a null signal. Public evidence related to safety label changes is common, and strict event-level public RWE documentation is nontrivial. However, deeper methodological reporting is uncommon. Source and design information are often missing or broad, and explicit confounding, missing-data, sensitivity, and negative-control reporting are rare. That makes transparency and method under-reporting a likely secondary headline result, not just a limitation.
